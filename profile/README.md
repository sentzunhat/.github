# Sentzunhat

**Useful products. Clear foundations.**

Sentzunhat is an independent software company in Winnipeg, Manitoba. We build focused apps and open tools that give people more control over their data and technology.

Our work spans personal archives, developer tools, and human-directed AI workflows. We distinguish released products from prototypes and research.

[Website](https://sentzunhat.com/) · [All projects](https://sentzunhat.com/#projects) · [Public code](https://github.com/orgs/sentzunhat/repositories?q=visibility%3Apublic)

## What we're building

**[Mochilada](https://sentzunhat.com/projects/mochilada/)** is a local-first app for preserving, exploring, organizing, and exporting social media archives you have downloaded yourself. It is being prepared for a macOS-first release.

## How we work

- **Keep people in control.** Make important actions understandable and leave meaningful decisions with the person using the software.
- **Make ownership real.** Build privacy and portability into the product, rather than relying on promises alone.
- **Build the smallest truthful thing.** Be clear about what exists today and what is still being developed.
