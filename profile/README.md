# Sentzunhat

**Small tools. Thoughtfully made.**

Sentzunhat Corp. is an independent software company. We build focused products and reusable foundations that give people more control over their technology and data.

Our work brings together local-first software, clear application architecture, and human-directed AI workflows. We care about systems people can understand, information they can take with them, and products that solve a real problem without unnecessary complexity.

## What we're building

| Project | What it is | Current direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Mochilada](https://sentzunhat.com/projects/mochilada/) | A local-first app for preserving, exploring, organizing, and exporting your downloaded social media archives. | Preparing for a macOS-first release. |
| [HAWP](https://github.com/sentzunhat/human-ai-workflow-protocol) | An open-source, human-led way to shape AI-assisted work and carry its intent across tools and sessions. | Published. [Project overview](https://sentzunhat.com/projects/hawp/). |
| [Zacatl](https://github.com/sentzunhat/zacatl) | An open-source library for building practical services with a minimal, straightforward approach. | Published. [Project overview](https://sentzunhat.com/projects/zacatl/). |

We also share [research and prototypes](https://sentzunhat.com/#explorations) while keeping their status distinct from released products.

## How we work

- **Keep people in control.** Make important actions understandable and leave meaningful decisions with the person using the software.
- **Make ownership real.** Build privacy and portability into the product, rather than relying on promises alone.
- **Build the smallest truthful thing.** Be clear about what exists today and what is still being developed.
